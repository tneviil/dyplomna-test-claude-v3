We know what your supervisor will like

You will receive a high-quality finished term paper with a guarantee of originality and timely completion.

We take on the entire process of preparing a term paper: from assistance in choosing a topic and developing a plan to writing, formatting and editing. Each work undergoes internal quality control, proofreading and anti-plagiarism control, so you can be sure of its uniqueness and compliance with academic standards.

What does the service include?

01

Concept and plan development

Free selection of a topic (if it has not yet been approved) and preparation of a detailed work plan that meets the logic of the research and the requirements of the scientific supervisor.

02

In-depth bibliographic analysis

Review of at least 20–30 relevant sources, including monographs, scientific articles in professional publications, and foreign literature from the last 3–5 years.

03

Writing the theoretical section

Presentation of the main paradigms, analysis of the approaches of various scientists and formation of one's own theoretical basis for research.

04

Practical part and calculations

Conducting empirical research, calculating economic indicators, conducting statistical analysis, creating graphs, charts and tables in MS Excel or specialized software.

05

Formation of conclusions and applications

A summary of the results for each section and general conclusions confirming the achievement of the work's objective.

06

Anti-plagiarism check

Multi-level control of text originality, which excludes the use of methods of artificially inflating indicators.

How we work: stages of order fulfillment

1/ 5
  1. 1

    1. Submitting an application

    Leave a request on the website (via the order form) or contact us in a convenient messenger. You need to indicate the topic of the work, subject, page volume, desired percentage of uniqueness and deadline. Also provide methodological recommendations and teacher requirements, if any - this will help to accurately assess the task. Submitting an application does not oblige you to anything - this is just the first step to receiving a consultation and calculating the price.

  2. 2

    2. Order evaluation and confirmation

    We select the best author for the topic (all interested performers respond, and we choose the one who has the necessary qualifications and free time). Then the manager contacts you and informs you of the exact cost and deadline for the work. If everything suits you - we confirm the order and move on to the next step. (If not - we cancel without any questions).

  3. 3

    3. Writing a term paper

    The author carefully fulfills your order. At this stage, we maintain contact: the manager can clarify details or request additional materials (for example, an approved plan or source data for the practical part). You can also monitor the progress through your personal account or requests to the manager. If agreed, we will send you intermediate results (for example, a finished chapter) for review. After writing, all work undergoes an internal quality check and plagiarism check.

  4. 4

    4. Handover and payment

    When the term paper is ready, we will send you a fragment of the work for review or we will hold a presentation of the work. You will make sure that everything is done correctly. After that, you pay the remaining amount, and we will send you the full text of the term paper with all attachments. Together with the work, you will receive an anti-plagiarism report, as well as a presentation and speech for defense (if ordered).

  5. 5

    5. Warranty support and protection

    After receiving the term paper, you have time to review it yourself and show it to your supervisor. If you have any corrections, just send them to us, and the author will promptly correct everything. We will be in touch until the day of the defense to advise or correct any minor details, if necessary. As a result, you successfully defend your thesis and receive your degree! Don't forget to share the good news with us later 😃 and leave a review.
